# Refund and Return Policy
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-REFUND-001
Category: Orders and Refunds
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Return eligibility
Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible products. Products must be unused and returned with original packaging and accessories. Perishable goods, customized products, and products marked non-returnable are excluded unless applicable law requires otherwise.

## Requesting a return
1. Sign in to the ShopEase account.
2. Open My Orders.
3. Select the order and choose Request a Return.
4. Select a reason and submit the request.
The support team reviews the request and provides return instructions.

## Refund processing
After the returned item is received and inspected, an approved refund is initiated within 3–5 business days. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider and bank.

## Refund status
Customers can check My Orders for refund status. If a refund remains pending beyond the communicated processing period, contact support with the order reference.

## Restrictions and escalation
The AI assistant must not approve refunds, promise an outcome, or change order status by itself. It may explain policy and create an escalation request. Disputes, damaged items, rejected returns, and overdue refunds should be routed to an authorized human agent.
