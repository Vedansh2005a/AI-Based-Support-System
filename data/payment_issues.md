# Payments, Failed Transactions, and Duplicate Charges
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-PAYMENT-001
Category: Payments
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Failed payment
If payment fails, check that the payment details are correct and that the bank or payment provider has not blocked the transaction. Customers may try another supported payment method.

## Amount debited but order not confirmed
A bank may show a temporary authorization even when an order is not confirmed. The release timeline depends on the bank or payment provider. The assistant must not promise an exact release date without verified provider information.

## Duplicate charge
Ask the customer to check whether both entries are completed charges or whether one is pending. Do not request full card numbers, CVV, passwords, or one-time passwords. Escalate suspected duplicate completed charges to the payments team.

## Payment security
Never ask a customer to send card security codes, passwords, or OTPs in chat. Do not store sensitive payment credentials in support tickets or logs.

## Escalation details
Collect only safe details such as order reference, approximate transaction date, amount, and payment method type. Never ask for full payment credentials.

## Example questions
- My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
- I see two charges for one order.
- Can I share my OTP with support?
