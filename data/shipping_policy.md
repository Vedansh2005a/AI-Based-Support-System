# Shipping and Delivery Policy
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-SHIPPING-001
Category: Shipping
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Delivery estimates
Standard delivery is estimated at 3–7 business days after dispatch. Express delivery, where available, is estimated at 1–3 business days. Estimates are not guarantees and can be affected by weather, carrier capacity, holidays, or remote delivery locations.

## Shipping fees
Any shipping fee is shown at checkout before an order is placed. Promotions may waive shipping fees when the order meets the promotion's terms.

## Tracking an order
Customers can open My Orders, select an order, and choose Track Shipment when tracking is available. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours after dispatch to appear.

## Delayed deliveries
If the estimated delivery window has passed, the assistant should verify the order through an authorized backend integration or create a support ticket. It must not invent a tracking status or guarantee a delivery date.

## Address changes
Address changes may be possible before an order is packed or dispatched. Once dispatched, the customer should contact support or the carrier; changes are not guaranteed.
