## Before troubleshooting
Ask for the product category and a brief description of the problem. Do not request unnecessary personal data. Check the product manual and safety warnings before suggesting steps.

## Basic steps
1. Stop using the product if it is overheating, smoking, sparking, leaking, or appears unsafe.
2. Check the manufacturer's instructions and confirm the product is being used as directed.
3. For electronic products, check the power source and compatible cables only if safe to do so.
4. Restart or reset only when the manual recommends it and the customer understands the consequences.
5. If the problem persists, create a ticket and route it to product support.

## Safety restrictions
Never instruct customers to open mains-powered devices, bypass safety mechanisms, handle exposed wiring, or perform repairs that could cause injury. For urgent hazards, advise the customer to stop use and follow local emergency or manufacturer guidance.

## Warranty and repair
Warranty coverage depends on the product's specific warranty terms. Do not promise a replacement or repair until eligibility is verified.

## Example questions
- My product will not turn on.
- The device is overheating.
- Can I repair the product myself?
