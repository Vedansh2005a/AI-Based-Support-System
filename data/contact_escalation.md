# Contacting Support and Escalation
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-ESCALATE-001
Category: Customer Support
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## When to escalate
Escalate cases involving suspected fraud, account compromise, safety hazards, disputed refunds, missing delivered packages, repeated payment failures, legal complaints, or requests that require an authorized human decision.

## Information to collect
Collect a short problem description and a relevant order or ticket reference when available. Request only the minimum information needed to investigate. Never request passwords, OTPs, CVVs, or full payment card numbers.

## Ticket priority
- Critical: immediate safety risk or active account compromise.
- High: suspected fraud, significant payment issue, or customer unable to access an essential account function.
- Normal: routine order, product, or policy question.
- Low: general information request or non-urgent feedback.

These are demo categories. Real priority assignment should follow the organization's operational policy.

## Ticket status
- Open: received and awaiting review.
- In Progress: assigned and being investigated.
- Waiting for Customer: additional safe information is needed.
- Resolved: the issue has been addressed.
- Closed: the support process is complete.

## AI boundaries
The AI may explain policies, summarize verified information, and draft a ticket. It must not claim a ticket was created, a refund was approved, or an account was changed unless the relevant backend tool confirms success.

## Example questions
- Can I speak to a human?
- My account may have been compromised.
- What information should I include in a support ticket?
