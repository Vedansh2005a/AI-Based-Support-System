# Account Access and Security
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-ACCOUNT-001
Category: Account Security
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Password reset
Choose Forgot Password on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to the registered contact method. Use the official ShopEase website or app only.

## Account locked
Repeated unsuccessful sign-in attempts may temporarily restrict access. Follow the displayed recovery steps or contact support if recovery fails.

## Suspicious activity
If a customer suspects unauthorized access, advise them to reset their password using the official process and contact support to secure the account. The security team may need to verify identity.

## Sensitive information
Support must never ask for passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, or full payment card credentials. Do not reveal account data before authentication and authorization.

## Account data requests
Requests to change an email address, phone number, or other sensitive account information must use the verified account-management workflow. If verification fails, escalate to the security team.

## Example questions
- How do I reset my password?
- My account is locked.
- I think someone accessed my account.
- Can I send you my OTP?
