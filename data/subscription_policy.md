# Subscription, Renewal, and Billing Policy
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-SUBSCRIPTION-001
Category: Subscriptions
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Subscription details
The plan page and checkout confirmation display the plan price, billing frequency, renewal date, and applicable terms. These details may vary by plan.

## Renewal
Subscriptions renew according to the terms shown at purchase unless cancelled before the applicable cutoff. The assistant must check the account's actual plan details before stating a customer's renewal date or amount.

## Cancellation
Customers can open Account Settings, select Subscriptions, and follow the cancellation steps. If the option is missing or an error occurs, contact support.

## Refunds
Subscription refunds depend on the plan terms and applicable law. Do not promise a refund without checking the relevant terms and authorized decision.

## Billing disputes
For an unexpected charge, collect only safe information and route the case to billing support. Never request passwords, OTPs, or full card details.

## Example questions
- When will my subscription renew?
- How do I cancel a subscription?
- I was charged after cancelling. What should I do?
