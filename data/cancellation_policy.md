# Order Cancellation Policy
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-CANCEL-001
Category: Orders
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Cancellation eligibility
An order may be eligible for cancellation while it is awaiting processing. Orders that have been packed or dispatched may no longer be cancellable through the website.

## How to request cancellation
Open My Orders, select the order, and check whether Cancel Order is available. If it is unavailable, contact support for a review.

## Refund after cancellation
If payment was captured and cancellation is approved, the refund follows the refund policy and payment provider timelines. If an authorization was only placed, the bank may release it according to its own schedule.

## AI action restrictions
The assistant may explain cancellation steps but must not cancel an order unless the authenticated user is authorized and a trusted backend cancellation tool confirms the action. Require confirmation before any irreversible action.

## Escalation
Route disputed cancellations, already-dispatched orders, and payment discrepancies to human support.
