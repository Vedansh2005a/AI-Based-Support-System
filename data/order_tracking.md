# Order Tracking and Delivery Status
Company: ShopEase (fictional demo company)
Document ID: KB-TRACK-001
Category: Order Tracking
Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Finding tracking information
Sign in, open My Orders, select the order, and choose Track Shipment. Tracking is displayed when the carrier provides it.

## Tracking has not updated
Carrier scans can take time to appear. Allow up to 24 hours after dispatch for the first tracking event. If no update appears after that period, contact support.

## Delivered but not received
Check the delivery location, household members, reception desk, and any carrier delivery photo. If the package remains missing, report it to support promptly.

## Incorrect tracking information
Do not rely on an unverified tracking number supplied in a conversation. Use an authenticated order lookup integration or escalate to a support agent.

## Privacy
Only show order information to the authenticated customer or another explicitly authorized person. Do not reveal another customer's address, order contents, or tracking details.

## Example questions
- Where can I track my order?
- My tracking has not changed. What should I do?
- My order says delivered, but I did not receive it.
