## Warranty coverage
Warranty duration, covered defects, exclusions, and remedies vary by manufacturer and product. The product listing, warranty card, and manufacturer's terms are the authoritative sources for a particular item.

## Starting a warranty request
Contact support with the order reference, product name, purchase date if available, and a description of the issue. Support may request proof of purchase through a secure channel.

## Common exclusions
Damage from misuse, unauthorized modifications, accidents, or normal wear may be excluded depending on the warranty terms. Do not determine eligibility without checking the applicable product terms.

## Repairs
Use an authorized service center when required by the manufacturer. Do not recommend unsafe repairs or opening a product that could expose the customer to electrical, chemical, or mechanical hazards.

## AI restrictions
The assistant may summarize documented warranty terms but must not guarantee coverage or approve a repair or replacement without an authorized decision.
